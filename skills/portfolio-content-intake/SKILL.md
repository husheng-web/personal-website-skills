# Portfolio Content Intake

Turn supplied project materials into a decision-ready intake without inventing facts.

## Workflow

1. Inventory the supplied files, links, and stated facts. Distinguish direct evidence from claims that need owner confirmation.
2. Classify content into identity, context, problem, audience, process, contribution, outcomes, media rights, and localization.
3. Produce an approval brief with: publishable facts, qualified claims, missing evidence, and precise questions for the owner.
4. Recommend `ready`, `partial`, or `content-pending`; do not promote a status merely because media exists.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Do not infer roles, dates, team size, metrics, awards, research findings, usage rights, or English copy from visual material.
- Treat unpublished sources and filenames as evidence candidates, not public facts.
- For every media item proposed for use, require a source location, intended role, dimensions when known, alt-text direction, and permission/approval state.
- Keep unpublished material and owner questions out of public-facing copy.

## Deliverable

Use compact sections: `source inventory`, `approved facts`, `claims needing qualification`, `content gaps`, `media readiness`, `localization readiness`, and `recommended next action`. This skill audits and plans; it does not implement a page unless the user separately asks.
